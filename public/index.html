Our story

A truly fair pour
for every guest.

FairPour started in 2016 as a small café in Fairport's historic Cannery building. We had one goal: be the neighborhood's third place, somewhere between home and work where you can relax, get work done or catch up with people you love.

In 2019 we started roasting our own coffee in small batches and became FairPour Coffee Roasters. Today the same care goes into every bag, every cup and every plate at all three of our cafés.

  1. 2016FairPour Café opens in the Cannery, Fairport
  2. 2019Small-batch roasting begins. We become FairPour Coffee Roasters
  3. 2025New cafés open at Ironworks Ithaca & Neighborhood of Play, Rochester
